# Academy-first public homepage

## Decision

KHLIM Basketball Academy remains the primary public brand and conversion focus. Khero is a supporting mascot and a member gamification layer, including Khero Points.

## Homepage hierarchy

1. Academy-first hero and enrolment calls to action.
2. Player-development, standards, and family-visibility pillars.
3. Published academy programmes.
4. Achievements and player spotlight.
5. Supporting Khero story using approved poster artwork.
6. Public Khero Points teaser linking to the secure member portal.
7. Final enrolment call to action and footer.

## Media rule

The public hero must not present generated people as authentic KHLIM academy photography. Until verified academy training photography is available, the hero uses a polished abstract black-and-gold basketball visual treatment. Approved Khero campaign posters are optimized as WebP and are used only in the supporting Khero section.

## Khero rule

Khero should make the experience warmer and more memorable without replacing the academy, players, coaches, programmes, or real development outcomes as the central public story. Khero Points are introduced publicly, while balances, point activity, and rewards remain part of the authenticated member experience.
