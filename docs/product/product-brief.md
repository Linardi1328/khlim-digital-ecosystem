# Product Brief

**Status:** Draft  
**Product:** KHLIM Super App  
**Organization:** KHLIM Basketball Club

## Problem

Youth basketball development involves more than training sessions. Players need clarity on schedules, progress, competitions, selections, and opportunities. Parents need reliable oversight of their children's commitments and development. Coaches need efficient tools for attendance and evaluations. Club administrators need a consistent way to coordinate all of this without depending on fragmented chats, spreadsheets, posts, and manual follow-ups.

KHLIM Super App aims to centralize those workflows while creating a stronger long-term relationship between athletes and the club.

## Purpose

Create one trusted digital hub for the KHLIM basketball journey, connecting club operations with player development, family supervision, communication, and engagement.

The app should help KHLIM:

1. Improve operational clarity around training, events, selections, and announcements.
2. Give players a visible record of their basketball development and participation.
3. Give parents appropriate oversight of schedules, attendance, progress, registrations, and coach communication.
4. Give coaches fast tools to manage rosters, attendance, evaluations, and development priorities.
5. Build stronger player engagement through KHERO identity, points, rewards, and achievements.
6. Create structured data that can support future analytics and human-supervised AI automation.

## Core product loop

> **Attend → Train → Improve → Earn → Participate → Repeat**

Training and development are the core. Gamification exists to reinforce participation and club engagement rather than distract from basketball.

## Primary users

### Player
Wants to know what is next, how they are improving, what they have earned, and what opportunities are available.

### Parent / Guardian
Wants to supervise one or more children, understand attendance and progress, respond to events, receive important updates, and contact appropriate coaches.

### Coach
Wants fast access to assigned sessions and players, efficient attendance, practical development tracking, and controlled communication with families.

### Club Administrator
Wants consistent control over users, teams, schedules, events, announcements, rewards, permissions, and operational reporting.

## Product principles

- **Development-first:** the product should strengthen athlete development rather than become a generic social app.
- **Family-aware:** parent/guardian functionality is core to the product architecture.
- **Coach-controlled evaluations:** coaches control official development assessments; players cannot self-edit official ratings.
- **Privacy by design:** information about minors must be minimized, access-controlled, auditable, and protected by default.
- **Modular architecture:** domains should remain loosely coupled so future features can be added or replaced safely.
- **Configurable business rules:** teams, evaluation categories, event types, point rules, and reward definitions should avoid unnecessary hard-coding.
- **Operationally realistic:** coach workflows must remain fast enough to use during real club operations.
- **Progressive complexity:** MVP proves the core workflows before advanced commerce, analytics, video, or AI are introduced.

## MVP value proposition

For the first public release, a KHLIM family should be able to open the app and understand:

- what training is coming up;
- whether the player has been attending;
- what the player is currently working to improve;
- what competitions, trials, or club events are approaching;
- whether the family needs to respond or register;
- what KHERO points and rewards the player has earned;
- which coaches are relevant and how to enquire about additional training.

A coach should be able to manage today's roster and player development without needing separate manual systems.

## Long-term opportunity

Over several years, the product can become a player's digital basketball identity inside KHLIM: teams played for, training history, competitions, selections, development milestones, achievements, KHERO progression, and coach-reviewed development plans.

This structured history can later support features such as:

- private training booking and payments;
- merchandise and rewards commerce;
- richer performance analytics;
- video and game-stat integration;
- parent progress summaries;
- coach workload automation;
- club operations agents;
- coach-approved AI development recommendations.

## MVP success signals

The MVP should be considered promising if:

- coaches consistently complete attendance;
- players regularly check schedules and progress;
- parents use the app for oversight and event actions;
- development evaluations are maintained instead of abandoned;
- important competition communication moves into the platform;
- KHERO points create measurable engagement;
- private coaching enquiries can be generated through the product;
- support burden and communication confusion decrease rather than increase.

## Non-goals for MVP 1.0

The MVP is not intended to include:

- a social network or public player feed;
- real-time direct messaging between all users;
- live match statistics;
- automated video analysis;
- wearable tracking;
- a full e-commerce marketplace;
- integrated payment processing;
- complex coach booking calendars;
- autonomous AI coaching;
- public leaderboards that rank children against each other.
