# Age-inclusive mobile UX principles

KHLIM public and guardian-facing experiences are mobile-first and must remain understandable to users who are less familiar with modern app conventions.

## Product rules

- Never make a logo, icon, swipe gesture, or hidden menu the only way to reach a critical destination or action.
- Pair unfamiliar icons with visible text whenever space allows, and provide explicit labelled alternatives for navigation and recovery actions.
- Keep primary actions visible instead of removing them at smaller breakpoints; reorganize them for mobile when necessary.
- Use plain, action-oriented wording and avoid relying on technical terminology where a simpler label is available.
- Give important interactive controls a touch target of at least 44px in height or width where practical.
- Maintain readable text sizes, strong contrast, clear focus states, and sufficient spacing between adjacent controls.
- Do not depend on hover for essential information or actions.
- Keep escape routes obvious on focused flows such as sign-in, registration, checkout, and onboarding.
- Preserve language selection at mobile widths and keep translations complete for every user-facing string.
- Test critical journeys at common phone sizes, including compact 320px widths and the primary 390x844 touch viewport.

## Acceptance mindset

A feature is not complete merely because an experienced smartphone user can discover it. Critical journeys should be understandable without prior knowledge of KHLIM, hidden gestures, or conventional logo-navigation behavior.
